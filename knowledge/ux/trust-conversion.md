# UX: Trust, Credibility & Conversion Psychology

## The trust stack (what users verify, in order)

1. **Does it look maintained?** current year in footer, fresh dates,
   working links, no broken images
2. **Is it real?** addresses, phone numbers, named people, photos of
   actual staff/office, company registration numbers (MENA/EU show these
   proudly)
3. **Is it safe?** HTTPS padlock era is over — users now check payment
   logos, policy links, recognizable seals (visa/mastercard/mada logos;
   regulatory badges in finance)
4. **Do others vouch?** reviews with substance, case studies with numbers,
   press logos, community size — specificity = credibility
5. **Will it be fair?** clear pricing, return policies, guarantees stated
   before the ask

## Specificity law

"We serve 4M customers" (dated: "as of 2025") beats "millions of happy
customers". Testimonial: "Cut invoice processing from 6 hours to 20
minutes — Sarah, CFO at [real company]" beats "Amazing product!".
Design surfaces for specifics: stat rows with sources, review histograms,
case-study metric cards.

## Conversion patterns that respect users

- **Risk reducers** near CTAs: free trial terms, no-card-required, cancel-
  anytime, money-back, free returns, free cancellation (travel's #1 chip)
- **Commitment gradient**: small ask first (email → demo → trial → paid);
  multi-step forms with progress beat walls of fields
- **Choice architecture**: 3 pricing tiers (anchor high, target middle),
  "most popular" social proof on target, feature parity tables
- **Urgency only when true**: real inventory counts, real deadlines
  (Ramadan sale ends Thursday — true), real cohort start dates
- **Payment localization** (MENA critical): COD, mada, Fawry, STC Pay,
  installments (Tabby/Tamara BNPL) — logos visible pre-checkout

## Friction audit (the checklist)

- Can I see the price without contact forms?
- Can I try/see the product without signup?
- Can I contact a human in ≤2 clicks (WhatsApp counts in MENA)?
- Is my cart/progress preserved if I leave and return?
- Does the page work before all third-party scripts load?

## Dark patterns — never (legal + ethical floor)

Fake countdowns, hidden subscription traps, confirm-shaming ("No, I hate
savings"), disguised ads, pre-checked upsells, obstruction on cancellation.
EU DSA/DMA and regional consumer protection increasingly enforce; beyond
law — trust is the asset.

## Social proof placement map

- Hero-adjacent: logo wall (B2B) / rating summary (commerce)
- Decision block: review count + verified badge
- Post-CTA: testimonial with specifics
- Footer: certifications + press

## Arabic/MENA trust signals

- WhatsApp button with number visible (response-speed culture)
- Arabic testimonials with real names/photos; family/tribal endorsement
  norms (group legitimacy)
- Government/regulator logos where applicable (ministry licenses in
  finance/health)
- Bilingual policies: Arabic = the legal reference version (label it)
- Local maps (Google Maps acceptable globally; verify Saudi/EG mapping
  products per client)

## Anti-patterns

- Trust badge walls with 12 unrecognized seals (dilution)
- Stock-photo testimonials; round-number-only stats ("1M+ users" with no
  date)
- Popups before content (kills both trust + Core Web Vitals)
- Hiding the company behind the product (who runs this?)
