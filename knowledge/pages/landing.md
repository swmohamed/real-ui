# Page Type: Landing Page (Campaign, Feature, PPC)

A single-intent page: one audience, one promise, one action. Unlike the
homepage, it must NOT route — it must convert or capture.

## Anatomy (the proven section order)

1. **Match block**: headline continues the ad/link promise (message match =
  #1 conversion factor), 1-line subhead, primary CTA + secondary (demo/
  video), hero visual showing the product in use
2. **Problem/agitation**: the user's world before (short, concrete)
3. **Solution demo**: product screenshots, interactive demo, video —
   artifacts beat claims
4. **Proof**: logos, testimonial WITH specifics ("cut onboarding from 3
   weeks to 2 days"), numbers
5. **Mechanism**: how it works (3–4 steps) — reduces perceived risk
6. **Objection handling**: pricing hint, security, integrations, FAQ accordion
7. **Final CTA**: repeat primary CTA with risk-reducer (free/no card/14 days)
8. Footer: legal + minimal nav (don't leak exits; but keep policy links —
   ad platforms require them)

## Copy rules that survive testing

- Headline formulas: outcome ("Ship features 2× faster"), audience-fit
  ("Payments for platforms"), or category-creation — in that order of
  safety. Subhead carries the qualifier.
- CTA verbs: start/try/get/build + object ("Start free trial",
  "ابرحلة الآن" variants in Arabic: ابدأ مجاناً). Avoid "Submit"/"Learn more"
  as primary CTAs.
- One CTA verb per page repeated; ghost-button secondary only.

## Observed/known patterns

- PPC landing = no global nav (or minimal) — exits kill ROAS; organic
  feature pages keep nav (SEO)
- Sticky CTA bar appears after hero scrolls out (SaaS standard) —
  desktop + mobile bottom bar
- Social-proof counters (live-ish "14 teams joined this week") — use only
  with honest data
- Long-form vs short-form: complex/B2B = long (3–6 screens); simple signup
  = short (2 screens max)

## Mobile

- CTA thumb-reach: bottom sticky bar with safe-area inset
- Video: poster + play (muted autoplay loop acceptable with motion-off
  respect)
- Forms: minimal fields (email → redirect to full signup)

## RTL/Arabic

- Message-match applies in Arabic: ad in Arabic → Arabic landing (never
  mix); RTL layout flips section order emphasis naturally (start=right)
- Testimonials: Arabic name+role+photo; company logos stay Latin — fine,
  logos are language-neutral
- Numbers localize (٥٠٪ vs 50%): pick Western digits for metrics
  consistency in Gulf/B2B, Arabic-Indic in Egypt consumer contexts

## Validation checklist

- [ ] One intent, one primary CTA verb repeated ≤4 times
- [ ] Proof artifact (screenshot/demo) above the fold-2
- [ ] Objection answers present (price/security/support)
- [ ] No leaky nav on paid traffic
- [ ] Load < 2.5s LCP on 4G (paid traffic = impatient traffic)
- [ ] Form ≤ 4 visible fields
