# Page Type: Homepage (by site archetype)

The homepage's job differs by archetype — design the RIGHT job, not "a hero."

## The 6 real homepage jobs

1. **Routing** (portals, news, gov): distribute visitors to tasks/sections
   fast. Success = fewer pogo-sticks. Design: search + task tiles + dense
   section rivers. Hero optional; density is the virtue.
2. **Positioning** (SaaS, B2B): explain the product in 5 seconds, prove it,
   route to trial. Design: statement hero + proof bar + feature sections +
   CTA repetition.
3. **Emotion** (luxury, automotive, entertainment, travel): make them feel
   the promise. Full-bleed media, minimal text, one CTA.
4. **Merchandising** (ecommerce): balance promo urgency with catalog access.
   Search-first or deal-first depending on price positioning.
5. **Product surface** (tools, scores, weather): the product IS the page —
   widget-first, no marketing chrome.
6. **Discovery** (games, streaming, social): curated shelves — the homepage
   is a table of contents for desire.

## Universal homepage structure (adapt ratios, keep order)

1. Orientation (masthead: who/where am I, language/currency)
2. Primary intent capture (search/task/CTA/hero)
3. Proof or content (the meat: shelves, features, rivers)
4. Secondary intents (links to deeper trees)
5. Trust/footnote layer (footer: policies, contact, social)

## Observed patterns (corpus)

- **h1 = exactly 1** (corpus average 1.0) — usually the positioning
  statement or the site name on routing pages.
- Above-the-fold variants that work: statement+CTA (SaaS), search widget
  (travel/ecommerce/marketplaces), poster hero + play CTA (streaming),
  live scores strip (sports), task grid (gov).
- Proof bar placement: immediately after hero on B2B/SaaS (logo wall +
  number); as footer-adjacent trust strip on ecommerce (delivery/returns/
  payments); never on luxury (brand is the proof).
- Personalized rows appear on return visits (continue/recently viewed) —
  above generic shelves.
- News homepages: lead cluster = 1 hero story + 4–8 secondary cards in a
  disciplined grid; most-read rail; live strip on top when warranted.

## Mobile transformations

- Hero text scales but promise doesn't: keep statement ≤2 lines at 390px
- Search-first archetypes keep search visible without expand-tap when
  search >50% of intent (marketplaces)
- Shelf rows become swipe rails with peek affordance
- Task grids become 2-col icon tiles; footer links accordion

## RTL notes

- Hero statement text-align: start (not left)
- Shelf rails scroll right→left naturally in RTL (scroll-snap handles it)
- Logo placement: Arabic-first brands commonly keep logo right-aligned
  with nav mirroring; observe per-brand (no single rule — be consistent)

## Common mistakes

- Hero that sells the company instead of the user's outcome
- 5 competing CTAs; carousel heroes (users interact with slide 1 only —
  engagement decays per slide, well-documented)
- Homepage as art piece with no routing (creative industry exception:
  portfolio home = work samples, still one click to contact)
- Burying language/currency switching in footer on international sites
