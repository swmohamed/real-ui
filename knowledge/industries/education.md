# Industry: Education & EdTech

## Characteristics
Two audiences: learners (motivation-sensitive) and institutions/parents
(buyers). Outcomes are the product; proof = completion + results + employers.
Motivation design (streaks, progress) is structural, not decorative.

## User intents
1. Learn a skill (course/program discovery)
2. Evaluate quality (instructor, syllabus, reviews, certificates, price)
3. Start/continue learning (player, exercises, progress)
4. Prove learning (certificates, assessments)
5. Teach/administer (instructor dashboards, LMS)

## Business goals
Enrollments/subscriptions (B2C), seat licenses (B2B/2School), completion →
renewal, institutional procurement cycles.

## Information architecture
- Consumer: Home (value + catalog preview) → Browse (subject tree) → Course
  page (syllabus, instructor, reviews, preview lessons, price/enroll) →
  Learning player (video + transcript + exercises + notes) → Certificates
- MOOC OBSERVED (Coursera): `--cds-` token family, degree vs course vs
  professional-track IA, enterprise tab, search-first
- Duolingo-class apps: unit path maps, XP/streak systems, hearts (lives)
- Institutional: program finder, accreditation pages, faculty, admissions
  funnels (multi-step applications with document uploads)

## Navigation
- Browse by subject (Business, Data, Design…) + level filters (Beginner/
  Professional) + language filters (Arabic courses as a facet in MENA)
- Persistent "My Learning" for logged users; cart for paid catalogs
- Almentor OBSERVED (AR video-learning): Inter + Noto Kufi Arabic pairing,
  Material icons, search + category rails, subscription CTAs (اشترك)

## Components that define the genre
- Course cards: provider logo, title, level chip, rating + count, duration,
  price/audit tag, "bestseller" flags
- Syllabus accordions (modules → lessons with durations + preview locks)
- Instructor panels with credentials + follower counts
- Learning player: chaptered video, speed, captions, transcripts with
  click-to-seek, note-taking, downloadable resources
- Progress: module completion rings/checklists, streak calendars
- Certificates with share/verify links (LinkedIn integration)
- Pricing: subscription vs per-course toggle; financial-aid links (MOOC
  convention); cohort start dates (bootcamp convention)

## Visual characteristics
- Consumer: bright friendly accents on white (Coursera blue #0056D2 OBSERVED
  theme-color), rounded 8–12px, illustration-tilted for kids/language
  (Duolingo green/featherweight illustration), 3D-mascot energy
- Institutional/professional: deep navy/burgundy heraldry, serif display,
  dense degree tables
- Arabic platforms: Kufi headers for modern energy (Almentor OBSERVED),
  Naskh body for long reading (Edraak OBSERVED Naskh-class reading)
- Imagery: real learners/instructors > stock; kid products use illustration

## Interaction patterns
- Preview lessons free (try-before-enroll standard)
- Quiz/exercise loops with instant feedback + explanation
- Progress persistence across devices; resume banners ("Continue where you
  left off")
- Cohort/community: discussion forums per course, cohorts, mentor sessions
- Enrollment funnels with multi-step forms + payment plans

## Mobile patterns
- Downloadable lessons/offline; audio-mode for language learning
- Streak widgets on home; quick 5-minute session CTAs
- Kids: giant touch targets, no hamburger (visible tabs)

## Arabic/MENA considerations (OBSERVED Edraak/Almentor)
- Arabic catalog facets: subtitle/dubbed language filters; religious-
  studies categories on generalist platforms
- Women-oriented learning verticals exist (segment awareness)
- Pricing sensitivity: free/audit + local payment (Fawry, STC Pay) +
  installment display
- Teacher-centered trust: instructor photos/bios in Arabic carry more
  weight than institution badges (talent culture)
- Bilingual certificates (name in Arabic + English) for employer sharing

## Conventions (follow)
Subject-tree browse, rich course pages with preview, syllabus accordions,
ratings-with-count, certificates, progress/resume, financial-aid honesty,
instructor credibility panels.

## Overused/anti-patterns
- Generic "unlock your potential" heroes with stock graduation caps
- Locking syllabi behind signup
- Autoplay video with sound on course pages
- Gamification without learning-science (points for watching = hollow)
- Hiding total price/hours commitment

## Strong references
Coursera (OBSERVED), Khan Academy, Duolingo, edX, Medium-class publishing
adjacent, Edraak (AR OBSERVED), Almentor (AR OBSERVED), Udemy (INFERRED),
universities (INFERRED class).

## Decision guidance
Learner-motivation products: friendly bright systems, progress mechanics,
player excellence. Institutional: heraldic serif trust, dense accurate
tables. Arabic: Kufi energy or Naskh readability depending on audience age,
instructor-led trust, local payments.
