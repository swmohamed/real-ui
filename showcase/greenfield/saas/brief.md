# Relay Desk product brief

Relay Desk is a support handoff console. Agents triage a team inbox, read a conversation, reply, assign ownership, and inspect customer and system context before resolving a case. Inbox, queue, conversation, message, customer, SLA, and operational signal drive the interface. Desktop keeps queue, conversation, and customer context aligned; phone makes the conversation primary and turns context into task destinations. AI drafting and sentiment scoring are intentionally absent.

- Research used: [Atlassian navigation guidance](https://atlassian.design/components/navigation-system/) for persistent product context and [WCAG reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html) for the phone handoff.
- REAL-UI knowledge used: `industries/saas-dev.md`, `devices/desktop.md`, `ui/data-display.md`, and `accessibility/floor.md`.
- Major decision: organize the screen around message chronology and customer context, not a KPI dashboard or AI-assistant panel.
