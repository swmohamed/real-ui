# Copper Fig product brief

Copper Fig is a neighborhood restaurant page built around tonight's service. Users confirm opening context, scan an allergen-aware menu, and reserve an available table. Service, menu section, dish, dietary marker, seating time, and reservation are the core entities. Desktop places booking beside the live menu; phone prioritizes menu reading and exposes Reserve as a persistent destination. Delivery, loyalty, and event ticketing are outside scope.

- Research used: [WCAG target size](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html) and [reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html) for menu/booking controls and the mobile service strip.
- REAL-UI knowledge used: `industries/restaurants-food.md`, `pages/account-settings-forms.md`, `ux/content-design.md`, and `input/touch.md`.
- Major decision: let today's menu and service facts lead; booking is adjacent on desktop and a labeled task on phone.
