# Groundwork product brief

Groundwork publishes document-backed local investigations. This page's job is sustained reading with inspectable evidence, not headline browsing. The core entities are investigation, chapter, dataset, source document, annotation, and correction. Desktop supports chapter orientation and an evidence rail beside the story; phone keeps the article linear and provides explicit Story, Evidence, Method, and Search destinations. Membership and advertising are not part of the modeled product.

- Research used: [BBC GEL typography](https://bbc.github.io/gel/foundations/typography/) for readable editorial hierarchy and [WCAG reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html) for the narrow reading sequence.
- REAL-UI knowledge used: `industries/news-media.md`, `ux/content-design.md`, `foundations/visual-hierarchy.md`, and `responsive/adaptive-models.md`.
- Major decision: make evidence inspection part of the article model instead of adding a generic related-story grid.
