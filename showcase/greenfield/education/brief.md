# Field Notes product brief

Field Notes is a self-paced practical data course. Users resume a learning path, study the current lesson, answer a check, record notes, and ask for a hint. Course, module, lesson, exercise, note, and progress are the core entities. Desktop holds path, lesson, and notebook in a study workspace; phone makes the current lesson primary and retains labeled access to path, notes, and help. Grading, certificates, and live tutoring are not modeled.

- Research used: [GOV.UK task-list guidance](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/task-list/) for clear status and next-task choice, applied only because this product has a real sequenced path.
- REAL-UI knowledge used: `industries/education.md`, `ux/content-design.md`, `ui/components.md`, and `responsive/adaptive-models.md`.
- Major decision: keep lesson, practice, and notes in one study context on desktop while turning them into explicit phone tasks.
