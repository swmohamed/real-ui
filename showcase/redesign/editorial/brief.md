# Editorial FULL redesign brief

Civic Wire publishes a daily general-news edition plus live updates. The
baseline is credible and usable, but its hero, uniform story grid, latest rail,
most-read list, and desk cards treat different reader questions as variants of
the same card surface.

The redesign becomes an edition briefing: date/desk orientation, a numbered
priority index, one contextual lead package, a chronological update wire, and
text-led desk lists. This preserves section browsing, latest, stories, live
updates, search, topics, and edition context while changing their
representations. Ads, paywalls, comments, personalization, and newsletter
capture remain outside scope.
