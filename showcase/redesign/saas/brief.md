# SaaS FULL redesign brief

Relayboard is an existing team-work product, not a marketing site. Its top job
is triage, followed by updating task status/ownership and reviewing deadlines.
The baseline is a plausible dashboard—welcome strip, KPI cards, kanban board,
activity cards, quick actions—but it makes overview chrome precede the work.

The new model is a queue-centered workbench: persistent project/view context,
an aligned priority list, a selected-task inspector, and a compact schedule
horizon. Drag remains optional because every move has an explicit control.
Phone navigation exposes Queue, Projects, and Schedule, with task detail as a
focused view. AI, billing, chat, time tracking, and new analytics are excluded.
