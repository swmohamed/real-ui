# Gaming FULL redesign brief

Prompt: a normal REAL-UI FULL REDESIGN request with no layout suggestion.

The source product is the existing bilingual El3abha browser-game project.
Its real capabilities are play, discovery, resume, local progression, a daily
challenge, personal scores, search, and Arabic/English routing. The failed
post-V4 output kept a split challenge hero, advertising band, continuation
rail, filtered card grid, and category rail while changing mainly skin and
proportions.

The re-derived priority is the active play session, not promotion. The new IA
is Play, Library, Challenge, and Scores. Home becomes a session workspace:
resume/current play first, quick switching beside it, then compact challenge
and score ledgers. On phone those jobs become labeled task views rather than a
stacked copy of the desktop sections.

Advertising, accounts, subscriptions, global social ranking, and a
recommendation engine are outside implemented scope. Full decision evidence,
capability dispositions, and the style-blind structure are in `case.json`.
