# Field Supply redesign brief

The product is a technical outdoor catalog, not a campaign page. Its redesign begins with trip intent, then supports narrowing, attribute comparison, variant choice, pickup availability, and cart continuity. The public fixture deliberately excludes membership and recommendations because neither is supported by product evidence.

The controlled baseline uses the common campaign/category/promotion/card composition found in the audited surface. The proposed implementation changes all nine style-blind structural dimensions and must pass `validate_redesign.py` before and after browser rendering.
