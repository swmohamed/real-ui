## Gate (this upgrade)

Not a new industry. Education already existed. The job was to store
**family + platform + learning-model decision logic** from real mobile
apps and real websites so later design does not collapse “course” into
one template.

Real products are evidence, not design authority. No templates, no
showcase, no demo apps.

≠ professional subscription ≠ interactive practice ≠ open courseware ≠
instructor-owned platform ≠ cohort school ≠ tutoring marketplace ≠
language-habit/exchange ≠ exam-prep/live school ≠ just-in-time scan ≠
study tools ≠ classroom LMS ≠ corporate L&D/LXP ≠ certification path ≠
kids live classes.

Plus prior: Arabic MOOC, skills-data, Scratch make/share.

and notifications. Copy the **object of work**, not a verticalized
homepage. Full decision table: `industries/education.md`.

and player models are multiple valid options. Progress/gamification is
family-conditional. Modern/professional means family-appropriate density
and proof — not a purple education kit. Anti-generic list extended.
