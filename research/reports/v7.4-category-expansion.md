## Gate (unchanged)

A new industry module is allowed only when both are true:

1. 20+ fetch-retained real production products (strict ledger class).
2. A distinct jobs / IA / risk split versus existing modules — not 20
   variants of one architecture.

IR shells, app-store landings, parked or hijacked domains, and thin JS
sites do not justify a module. Rejected spaces become families inside
the module they actually match. Rejected `candidate-*` tags are **not**
blanket-mapped into existing floors.

## Approved modules

### insurance

Banks move money you hold. Insurers price a contingent promise.
Families: direct quote · aggregator · takaful · regional binder · group IR.
Takaful is not `islamic-apps.md`. AXA and Prudential move off
`finance-banking` (32 → 30). Ledger row: **41** (includes IR shells and
Porch, which now serves home insurance). Thin this wave: AAMI empty nav,
Direct Line empty, Allstate maintenance — count, do not invent quote IA.
Tawuniya / GIG KSA fetch-failed — do not invent an Arabic quote wizard.

### telecom

Consumer plan / device / prepaid shops. Not a civic portal and not a
consulting IR tree. Families: consumer shop · locale-first / RTL shop ·
operator + payments · group IR. Verizon and STC move off
`government-public` (26 → 24 before public-law retags). Ledger row: **42**.
Thin this wave: AT&T, Airtel, Singtel, Vodacom, du, Optus, Spark, KT,
giffgaff empty or near-empty. Do not copy STC's 612-link enterprise tree
onto a prepaid MVNO. Do not treat group.vodafone.com as the consumer product.

### local-services

Hiring a person for a local physical job, or booking a local appointment.
Not a job board, not food delivery, not a property listing. Families:
task/trades marketplace · on-demand home super-app · appointment
marketplace · pro/field SaaS (Housecall Pro, Jobber, Mindbody) as a
**family**, not a fifth category. Filename is `local-services.md`.
Ledger row: **28** after skipping Kaodim (hijacked to betting) and
refiling Porch. Thumbtack / Trade Me / Localsearch are thin JS — counted
as products, no invented IA.
